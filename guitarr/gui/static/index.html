Calibrate latency

You'll hear 4 clicks to catch the pulse and then 12 more. Hit the muted strings (a short pick stroke) right on every click. Use headphones.

Tips

  • Your level should be between −30 and −10 dB while playing. If it turns red, lower the gain.
  • A clean signal (no distortion) works best; monitor your effects separately.
  • If soft attacks aren't detected, raise the sensitivity or lower the noise gate.
